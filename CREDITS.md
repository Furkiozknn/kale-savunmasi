## Stylised 3D chibi images (`assets/3d/`, 23.11.2026)

Made for this game in the style of its song video, with the Gemini app (Nano Banana image model, free tier), using a
frame of the video as the style reference. Two 2x2 sheets on white were generated, then cut out locally (PIL, white
flood fill) and saved as webp.
